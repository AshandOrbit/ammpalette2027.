# Naming and numbering reconciliation

Rule: one canonical name and one canonical numeral per card. Printed labels on the artwork are recorded separately in data/cards.json (printed_label) and flagged needs_relabel. Names marked provisional need Amy's approval.

## Worlds and decks

Six worlds: Estuary, Forest, Ocean, Abyss, Mountain, Arctic (the earlier Four Worlds copy covered four of these). Deck names: Estuary Tarot, Sylvan Tarot, Oceanic Tarot, Abyss Tarot, Alpine Tarot, Arctic Tarot. Woodland is Forest, Coastal is Estuary, Alpine is Mountain, Abyssal is Abyss.

## Decisions

| Item | Was | Canonical | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Neritus (Moon XVIII) | Abyss in text | Estuary Moon | Art is the sea purslane estuary card. Abyss Moon is open (suggested: Bathys, provisional). |
| Altara | Hermit in text, Mountain VIII on art | IX Hermit | Removes the clash with Strength VIII. Art needs relabel. |
| Sylvan | Emperor in text, Forest XV Sylva/Sylvana on art | IV Emperor, Sylvan | Removes the clash with Devil XV. Art needs relabel. |
| Estuary Temperance XIV | Alba (plant table), Delfin (dolphin art) | Alba | Keeps the plant correlation. Delfin art is an animal variant, not used. |
| Estuary Death XIII | Coralia | Rhoda (provisional) | Coralia is too close to Corallia (Ocean Empress). |
| Estuary Devil XV | Nereus | Fucus (provisional) | Nereus is Ocean Strength VIII. |
| Ocean High Priestess II | Pelagia | Thalassa (provisional) | Pelagia is the name of your verification architecture. Keep it only if you want that overlap. |
| Ocean Death XIII | Transformation | Pterois (provisional) | A concept word sat in the name slot. |
| Abyss Death XIII | Resurgence | Basalta (provisional) | Same. |
| Arctic names | Pack Ice, Tundra, Glacier, Resilience, Transformation, Renewal, Interconnection | Nilas, Dryas, Serac, Monodon, Calva, Sterna, Borea (provisional) | First four were places, last four were concepts. Aurora kept. |
| Arctic titles | "The Magician" etc. | Magician, High Priestess, Empress, Emperor, Star, World | Printed titles omit "The". |
| Estuary Emperor IV Duarte | Alpine backdrop | Duarte, redraw | Art does not match the world. |
| Duplicate art | Bram (two bears), Aves (two herons), Gaia (dolphin and turtle) | First sheet image used, others held as alternates | One card per slot. Bram is Sylvan Strength. |
| Sea purslane | Halimione portulacoides | Atriplex portulacoides | Current accepted name. Verify before publishing. |
| Ocean Oracle | 78 cards | Count not set | Tarot has 78. An oracle deck has no fixed count. |
